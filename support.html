Need help with Patronus Home?

Most settings can be changed directly from the extension. Turn on Edit, then open your profile/settings button to update your name, spirit artwork and shortcut preferences.

Common questions

How do I add a shortcut?
Turn on Edit mode, open the shortcut tab you want, and use the Add shortcut control.

How do I create another shortcut tab?
Turn on Edit mode and use the + control next to your current tabs.

How do I change my name or spirit artwork?
Turn on Edit mode, click your profile button, and open Settings.

How do I pause the animations?
Click the Ⅱ button in the top-right corner. Click it again to resume them.

How do I remove Patronus Home?
Open Chrome's extension management page, find Patronus Home and choose Remove.
